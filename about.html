ABOUT RCTV

What is RCTV?

RCTV is an IPTV (Internet Protocol Television) service that delivers live television and entertainment over an internet connection rather than through traditional cable or satellite.

RCTV provides a convenient way to access available entertainment on compatible devices such as Amazon Fire TV / Fire Stick and Android-based devices. The service is designed to bring live channels and on-demand content together in one easy-to-use platform.

Live TV

Access available live television channels through the RCTV service using a compatible device and internet connection.

Video on Demand

Browse available movies and other on-demand entertainment whenever you want to watch.

Multiple Connections

RCTV offers plans for customers who want to use the service on more than one compatible device.

Fire TV & Android

RCTV applications are available for supported Amazon Fire TV / Fire Stick and Android-based devices.

RCTV VPN

An optional VPN service is available for customers who want an additional layer of privacy for their internet connection.

Support & Announcements

Customers can access setup information, troubleshooting assistance and service announcements through the RCTV website and WhatsApp Community.

How IPTV Works

Instead of receiving television through a traditional cable box, IPTV delivers television through an internet connection.

Internet Connection → RCTV App → Live TV & On-Demand Entertainment

A stable, sufficiently fast internet connection is important for reliable streaming. Available programming may vary depending on the service and subscription.
