Troubleshooting Tips

Please complete these steps prior to contacting support:

  1. Check the RCTV Announcement Community to see if an issue has been reported.
  2. Clear Cache.
  3. Restart your Fire Stick.
  4. Restart your internet router and any wireless access points/extenders.
  5. Log out, then log back in.
  6. Refresh Channels.
  7. Clear Data only after contacting support. This normally resolves many issues, but it requires re-entering your credentials and redoing your favorites.

How to Clear Cache / Data

  1. Go to Settings on the main Fire Stick page.
  2. Go to Manage Applications.
  3. Scroll until you see RCTV.
  4. On the right, you should see Clear Cache and Clear Data. Press OK, then choose the appropriate option.
  5. Try Clear Cache first. Clearing cache does not remove your username.
  6. After clearing cache or data, return to the RCTV app.
  7. If you cleared data, select Allow when prompted for access to pictures/files.
  8. Enter your username and password. The Playlist name can be anything you choose.
